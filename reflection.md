# Reflection

## What I built

An interactive single-page visualizer comparing two string-comparison strategies: a vulnerable
early-exit comparison that stops at the first mismatched character, and a fixed constant-time
comparison that always does the same amount of work. Typing a guess updates a live bar chart
showing how much simulated work the comparison does per character position, and an "auto-attack"
button runs a simulated timing attack that recovers a secret character-by-character.

## What I learned / how it deepened my understanding

Our main takeaway as a team was less about any single line of code and more about the process
itself: how to actually use AI assistance well on a security-focused assignment like this one.
Working through scripting and app logic with AI support, and deciding as a team where to direct
it versus where to figure things out ourselves, was the real skill we walked away with, on top of
the specific side-channel concept the app demonstrates.

## My build workflow

We worked through scripts and app logic together as a team, using AI assistance throughout the
process. Part of what we got out of this sprint was learning how to use AI effectively for
security-related work: knowing what to ask for, reviewing what came back, and iterating on the
visualization's logic and layout until it matched what we wanted the app to teach.
